Country Camping Portal

Designing an immersive wilderness booking ecosystem and lifestyle brand identity for premier glamping and off-grid mountain expeditions.

Timeline

The wild, meticulously crafted.

The Objective: Country Camping wanted to disrupt standard travel directories by offering an experiential booking engine that visually conveys the crisp pine air, serene starlight, and luxury off-grid comfort of their private campsites.

The MDZ Approach: We engineered an intuitive date and terrain booking matrix paired with high-definition drone footage and micro-weather widgets. The interface allows adventure-seekers to customize their tents, firepit setups, and guided trails in under two minutes.
